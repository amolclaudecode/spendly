Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Last updated: May 2026

Data We Collect

Spendly collects only the information you provide when using our app:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you register
  • Expense data: Amount, category, date, and description for each expense you log
  • Usage data: Basic information about how you interact with the app to help us improve

How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve our expense tracking service:

  • To maintain your account and provide access to the app
  • To log and organize your expense records
  • To generate spending summaries and category breakdowns
  • To communicate with you about your account when necessary

We never sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device and on our secure servers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Secure data transmission using industry-standard encryption
  • Password hashing using strong cryptographic algorithms
  • Regular security reviews of our systems

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for:

  • Hosting: To store and serve your data securely
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our app (aggregated, anonymized data only)

These providers are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.
